## 📖 What Is Proofodds?

Proofodds is a **free, public experiment** that shows you whether football match predictions can actually beat the world’s most accurate betting line — **Pinnacle’s closing odds**.

Every week, before any match kicks off, we calculate the probability of home win, draw, and away win using a sophisticated statistical model (based on Dixon-Coles and Poisson distributions). Then, we **cryptographically seal those predictions** using a hash chain, so nobody can change them afterward. When the match ends, we compare our forecast against Pinnacle’s closing lineand publish the results — wins, losses, and everything in between.



The entire live record is **public and transparent**. We show you the good days and the bad days. No cherry-picking, no hidden data, no excuses. You can always see exactly how the model is performing right now.

.

## 🔧 What’s Under the Hood? (For the Curious)

While you don’t need to understand the code, here’s a simple explanation of what makes proofodds tick:



**1. Dixon-Coles + Poisson Model (🧠**

This is the mathematical engine behind the forecasts. It looks at historical scoring rates, team strength, and home-field advantage to estimate how many goals each team is likely to score, then converts those numbers into probabilities for win/draw/loss.



**2. Pinnacle’s Closing Line (🎯**

Pinnacle is widely considered the most accurate sportsbook in the world. Their closing line—the odds right before kickoff—reflects the collective wisdom of sharp bettors. We judge our model against this gold standard.



**3. Hash Chain Sealing (🔒**

Before each match, we createa unique digital fingerprint (hash) of our predictions and publish it publicly. After the match, we reveal the original predictions, and anyone can verify that they weren’t changed afterward. This guarantees **zero cheating** — the record is tamper-proof.



**4. Backtesting & Continuous Improvement (📈

We’ve run thousands of historical matches through the model to fine-tune its parameters. The live implementation is the final, honest test—no lookahead bias, no adjustments mid-season.

.

## 🔑 Hashes & Verification (For the Tech-Savvy)

For each match, we publish the SHA‑256 hash of the predictions **before** the match. After the match, you can verify:

```bash
echo "home_win=45.2% draw=28.1% away=26.7%" | sha256sum
```

Compare it to the published hash. If they match, the predictions were indeed sealed in advance. No one—not even us—can alter the historical recordonce it’s published.
